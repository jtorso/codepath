Tortilla Pizza — under $5 & ready in minutes

A live, hands-on workshop teaching college students how to make a low-cost, healthy tortilla pizza — crispy, melty, and customizable for dorm life.

1 Tortilla Tomato sauce / ketchup Shredded cheese Veggie toppings ~300–400 cal

Quick recipe:
Looking for a quick, cheap, and delicious meal? In this episode of College Cooking, we’re making a Tortilla Pizza that costs less than $5 and takes only minutes to make! Perfect for busy students, dorm living, or anyone on a budget.

  1. Lay 1 tortilla flat.
  2. Spread tomato sauce (or ketchup) to taste.
  3. Add shredded cheese and your favorite toppings.
  4. Bake in toaster oven or skillet until cheese melts and edges crisp.
  5. Slice & enjoy — fast, affordable, and seriously tasty.
Cost: $5 • Time: ~10 min
